Takeaway

Thriving in Cross-Functional, Fast-Paced Environments

→ Rapid project cycle

Shipping a project doesn’t end with design — after handing it off to engineers, it’s equally important to follow up on data and experiment results to understand the impact and drive improvements.

→ AB testing

AB Testing is a big thing for Tiktok, hence from this experience I learn a lot on keeping this in mind of design

→ Heavy multitasking

Throughout my internship, I consistently juggled 5–6 projects at once, making it essential to manage timelines and set clear priorities.
