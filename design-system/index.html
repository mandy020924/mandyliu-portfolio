01 · Principles

Four rules every page and every case study keeps

Grey speaks first, black answers

Headlines sit in #989898 with italic accents in #545454. Black is reserved for the nav ink, card titles and body copy, so it always reads as the answer, never the noise.

Everything scales from 1730

The Figma frame is 1730px wide. Any measure becomes fluid by dividing by 17.30: 44px of type is 2.5434vw, 158px of space is 9.1329vw. Clamp the ends, never the middle.

Motion arrives near to far

Things start close, slightly out of focus, and settle back. Ease-out curves only, blur-in for text, no rainfall. Nothing moves unless the reader did something or just arrived.

Inverse ink over dark

Fixed text, the nav and the footer row, is white in difference blend. It reads black over the white page and white over dark covers, with no per-page colour logic.

05 · Shape & depth

One radius family, and shadows that describe distance

Cards are 40px. Everything smaller is a fraction of that, everything round is a pill. Shadows have three layers: a tight contact shadow, a mid penumbra and a wide ambient one that grows as the card lifts.

06 · Blur

Progressive, never flat

Every blur on the site is a ramp of stacked backdrop bands, so an edge dissolves instead of frosting. No tint anywhere: the page colour is never painted over the blur.
