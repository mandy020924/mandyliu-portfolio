Hedra World

Redesigning the core of a 3D AI content creator, from a raw alpha to a workspace that shipped for testing, in three months.

  • Project impactCore features redesigned, 3 new functionalities shipped for testing
  • Timeline3 months
  • Role & teamProduct designer, with lead designer Payam and the Hedra engineering team
  • DomainGenerative AI, 3D, Creator Tools

When I joined Hedra, the team had a design agency’s proposal and a raw version of the app. As a designer fluent in 3D software and image-generation AI, I worked with our alpha users and artist-in-residence to find the pain points, then led the redesign of Hedra World’s core features and integrated three new functionalities in three months. The designs shipped for testing.

About the product

Hedra↗ World is a 3D AI content creator that gives creators full control over video and image generation.

Currently in alpha, Hedra builds worlds from scratch with text-to-3D generation, restyles scenes with Stable Diffusion in real time, and brings a powerful asset library and object animation into one tool. Over three months I collaborated closely with alpha users, artists-in-residence and engineers to redesign the core features and integrate new ones into the existing application, from 0 to 1.

Demo video by Hedra’s artist-in-residence, Martin, and our lead product designer, Payam.

Creative workflow

Build a world, design the scene, render it live.

01

World building

Build from scratch by importing assets from Hedra’s library, or generate them with text- or image-to-3D to populate the world.

02

Scene design

Design the 3D scene with composition and motion.

03

Rendering

Render the scene in real time with Stable Diffusion. The outcome can be a video or an image.

Building principles

A simple tool that helps creators unleash their imagination, rather than a sophisticated design tool

Casual to use

Usability first: Hedra World should feel like a simple tool that helps creators unleash their imaginations, not a professional suite to be learned.

01

Seamless world building

Interface redesign: fewer clicks and mode switches, with drag and drop that feels natural, so building a world is one continuous motion.

02

01 / Major changes

A new workspace structure

Working with our users and stakeholders, I proposed a workspace redesign to make scene building feel seamless with less friction, and to scale for the features still to come.

Before

Assets lived behind a library module, and the side panel stopped at the scene manager

Users had to leave the scene to import anything, and the panel had no room for features beyond the scene manager.

After

The asset library moved into the side panel, with drag and drop

World building became one continuous motion, and the panel expands to hold new features as they arrive.

02 / Core features

Improving the usability of Text to 3D and Diffusion Restyle with the people already using them

Hedra World’s core features focus on simplifying the workflow. My goal was to reduce friction by minimising clicks and mode switches, in a familiar drag-and-drop interface that needs little to no learning. The decisions came from working with our artist-in-residence on the previous version, and from becoming a user of similar tools and studying competitors.

Text to 3D

Assets from a sentence

Creating assets from scratch is Hedra’s most distinctive approach to world building: the user sketches roughly in a 3D canvas, with motion and composition from any angle.

Restyle with Stable Diffusion

A dedicated bottom bar, reachable at any stage

I gave Restyle prominence with its own bar, so its functionality is one step away whatever the user is doing.

Realtime preview

Compose on the left, see the diffused scene on the right

In split-window mode the user adjusts the composition and watches the changes apply to the restyled scene in real time.
