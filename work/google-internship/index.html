Google Internship

Designed how Gemini answers come to life on Google smart displays, turning text-heavy AI responses into glanceable, hands-free experiences for the home.

  • Project impact12+ answer templates for Gemini on smart displays
  • TimelineMay – Aug 2026
  • TasksUX Design, Prototyping, Design Systems
  • DomainSmart Home, Voice AI, Generative UI

At early access, Gemini on smart displays answered with walls of text that were hard to read from across the room. I re-architected the display's top use cases, visual answers, into glanceable, voice-first format. I built an API-integrated prototype backed by 12+ templates, an atomic component system, and strict LLM-to-UI generation rules, then stress-tested it with live Gemini responses to turn any answer into a hi-fi visual experience.

About the product

Gemini for Home↗ is Google's AI voice assistant for Nest speakers and smart displays.

Gemini for Home upgrades Google Assistant into a more conversational, capable helper. Say "Hey Google" for everyday tasks like timers, recipes, and smart home control, or "Hey Google, let's chat" for open-ended, back-and-forth conversation through Gemini Live.

Design Principle

An AI assistant that works from across the room

AI native

Gemini can say anything, so the design has to fit anything. Every layout is built to flex and is stress-tested against unpredictable AI responses.

01

Visual forward

Answers are built to be read at a glance, from a distance. Layouts adapt to proximity: bold, minimal information from across the room.

02

Hands-free first

Every interaction can be completed by voice. Your hands are full of flour or a phone is out of reach, so the screen never requires a tap to get the answer.
