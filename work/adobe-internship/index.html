Adobe Internship

Worked on an early-stage AI assistant for a web-based video editor, exploring how video editing can be more approachable.

  • Project impactAn AI styling assistant concept, prototyped and tested with 6 creators
  • TimelineJun – Aug 2025
  • TasksProduct Design, Prototyping, User Research
  • DomainVideo Editing, Generative AI, Creator Tools

I shaped the early direction of an AI video styling assistant for Adobe Firefly Video Editor through market research, AI capability analysis, iterative prototyping, and testing with six video editing enthusiasts. The work explored how AI could help creators turn video inspiration into an editable starting point.

About the product

Adobe Firefly Video Editor↗ is a browser-based video editing platform with generative AI built in.

The Firefly video editor (beta) is a browser-based editor for trimming clips, adding text and audio, and bringing Firefly-generated videos into your timeline.

Browser-based access and editing

Use the video editor directly in your web browser without the need to download or install any app.

In-built Firefly Video model

Use the Firefly Video model to generate videos or access your generation history to add videos to your timeline

Problem

Creators had inspiration. Getting from a reference to a first edit was the hard part.

A video reference can spark an idea, but translating its style into clips, text, and other editable assets takes time—and a blank timeline can make it hard to know where to begin.

Project goal

Help creators turn a video reference into a first edit—without taking away their creative control

How can we use AI to pull the style out of a video reference, such as color, text, and pacing, and turn it into editable assets creators can tweak, swap, or ignore?

Reference → Actionable item

Actionable item → Stylized edit
